12 · Movable Column Groups

With movableColumns and movableColumnGroups both on, a group header can be dragged like a column. Drag a group header to move the group among its siblings, or drag a column header to move that column within its group. A nested group moves only inside its parent. The option needs movableColumns. ← back to index

Job holds a nested Role group next to Salary: Role and Salary swap places inside Job, but Role cannot leave it.
